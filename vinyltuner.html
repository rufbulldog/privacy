VinylTuner Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

VinylTuner ("the app") measures a turntable's speed using your phone's camera. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission so it can watch a spinning record. Camera frames are analyzed in real time, entirely on your device, to calculate rotation speed. Images and video are never saved, recorded, or sent anywhere, and are discarded as soon as each frame is processed.

Information we collect

None. VinylTuner has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It doesn't access your location, microphone, contacts, photos, or files. Measurement results stay in the app's memory and are cleared when you close it.

Sharing results

If you tap Export, the app opens your device's standard share sheet with a text and CSV summary of your measurement. You choose where it goes (for example, email or a notes app). The developer doesn't receive it.

App updates

The app checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for bug-fix updates when it starts. That request includes only technical details needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version and platform, along with standard network information like your IP address. It doesn't include personal information. See Expo's privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
